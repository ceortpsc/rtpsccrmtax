# Badge and Disclosure Catalog

## Permitted UI labels when substantiated
- HTTPS / Secure Connection
- TLS certificate issuer and validity status
- Payment processing by Stripe
- Privacy notice available
- Terms available
- Electronic consent captured
- Accessibility statement available
- Security controls documented

## Restricted claims
Do not use:
- IRS Approved
- IRS Certified
- Treasury Approved
- IRS Secure
- DigiCert IRS Certified
- PCI Certified

unless the organization possesses the exact credential/certification and is authorized to make that representation.

## Footer disclaimer
"Payment processing is provided through the configured payment processor. Security certificates establish encrypted transport; they do not constitute tax, IRS, Treasury, legal, or regulatory approval. Government forms and requirements are subject to the authoritative issuing agency and applicable jurisdiction."
